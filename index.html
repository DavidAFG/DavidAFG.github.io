About 48.83° N / 2.34° E

A researcher working at the seam of spatial data and development economics.

I am finishing an MSc in Development Economics at Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne and working remotely as a consultant in the Regional Integration Unit at the Inter-American Development Bank, where I build sub-municipal databases for the Amazon and study the spatial organization of international trade.

Before Paris, I read economics at Tec de Monterrey, where I specialised in data science for the public good and co-authored work on inequality, taxation, and the economic value of care. My current research combines remote sensing, machine learning, and applied econometrics to recover geographic detail in places where official data is thin.

I work in Spanish, English, French, and (slowly) Japanese.
